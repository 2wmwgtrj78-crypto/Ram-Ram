SurgiMaster — NEET-SS (Dec 2026) + INI-SS (Mar 2027)
=====================================================

Upload the six files (index.html, SurgiMaster.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js,
icon.png, icon-512.png) to GitHub. Full walkthrough in GITHUB_SETUP.txt.


THE PLAN
--------
120 content days, Mon to Fri. 832 hours of work against 840 available.
  Playback 1.5x           449h of video becomes 300h
  Split 36 / 64           lectures / questions+review+repair
  1 hour buffer a day     carved out before the split
  6 hours every Saturday  catch-up slack, no new content ever
  Sunday                  repair sets and a pre-mortem
  11 mocks                every fourth Saturday, plus the tapers
  Two tapers              10 days before NEET-SS, 14 before INI-SS
  Two days off            after each paper

54% of the campaign lands before NEET-SS on 11 December. Because every topic is
interleaved from day one, that means roughly half of ALL 38 topics rather than
all of half — which is what NEET-SS breadth needs.


THE TWO PAPERS DIFFER, AND THE APP KNOWS IT
-------------------------------------------
  NEET-SS   150 q / 150 min   60 s each   penalty 1/4   3 locked sections
  INI-SS     80 q /  90 min   68 s each   penalty 1/3   one open paper

Blind guessing:
  NEET-SS   +0.06 a question. NEVER leave a blank.
  INI-SS     0.00 exactly. A blank costs you nothing.

Break-even accuracy is 20% for NEET-SS, 25% for INI-SS. The app computes YOUR
expected value at each confidence level from the calibration probe, so the
attempt rule on Progress is yours, not generic advice.

Yield weighting, mock format and pace target all switch to whichever paper is
next. Review intervals anchor to the nearer exam, so nothing is ever scheduled
to land after the paper it was meant for.


THE DAILY LOOP — NO ESSAY WRITING
---------------------------------
1. The bank block comes BEFORE the lecture. On a first pass it is a pretest;
   guess freely, being wrong is the point provided you read the answer.
2. Log attempted and correct. Two taps. This is what drives everything else.
3. Read every explanation and TALLY each miss: gap / could not reach it /
   mixed up / misread. One tap each. No typing.
4. Write up ONLY the answers you were certain about, capped at three a day.
   Those get a red card that will not clear until you retrieve them again the
   same day — feedback alone lets a confident error come back.
5. Five calibration taps: decide how sure you are BEFORE checking.
6. Ten minutes of blank-page recall to close.

Repair sets replace flashcards. The app tells you which topics to re-attempt
and how many, and you take them in DocTutorials with the "previously incorrect"
filter. A topic never returns inside 7 days, because below that you recognise
the answer instead of the reasoning.


WHAT PROGRESS SHOWS
-------------------
  Readiness       projected score with a band, against the 50% qualifying line
  Attempt rule    your own EV at certain / unsure / guessed
  Syllabus grid   all 38 topics coloured by accuracy
  Marginal gain   where the next hour recovers the most marks
  Calibration     accuracy at each confidence level
  Consistency     one square per day of the campaign


BACKUPS
-------
Log > Backup > Copy, into a note. Covers write-ups, scores, tallies,
calibration and repair history. Merging never deletes; scores only go up.

PUTTING SURGIMASTER ON YOUR IPHONE VIA GITHUB
=============================================
This is a one-off, about ten minutes. After it, the app sits on your home
screen with its own icon, opens full screen with no browser bars, and works
with no signal at all.

You need: a browser (phone or computer, either is fine) and an email address.
It is free. You are not writing any code — GitHub is only being used as a
place to put six files.


PART 1 — MAKE THE ACCOUNT
-------------------------
 1. Go to github.com and click "Sign up".
 2. Enter your email, make a password, pick a username.
    The username becomes part of your web address, so pick something you do
    not mind seeing — e.g. "sge-surg" gives sge-surg.github.io later.
 3. Verify the email they send you.
 4. Choose the FREE plan if it asks. Skip any questionnaire.


PART 2 — MAKE THE REPOSITORY
----------------------------
A repository ("repo") is just a folder on GitHub.

 5. Once signed in, click the "+" at the TOP RIGHT of the page, then
    "New repository".
 6. Repository name:  surgimaster
    (lower case, no spaces — this also becomes part of the address)
 7. Set it to PUBLIC.
    This matters: free GitHub Pages hosting only works on public repos. It
    means anyone with the exact link can view the app. That is fine — the app
    contains no personal data. Everything you log stays on your phone and is
    never uploaded.
 8. Tick "Add a README file".
 9. Click "Create repository".


PART 3 — UPLOAD THE FILES
-------------------------
10. On your new repo page, click "Add file" (a button near the top right of
    the file list), then "Upload files".
11. Drag in ALL SIX files from this folder:
        index.html
        SurgiMaster.html
        manifest.webmanifest
        sw.js
        icon.png
        icon-512.png
    On a phone: tap "choose your files" and select them from Files.
    Upload all six together. Do not put them inside a subfolder — they must
    sit at the top level of the repo.
12. Scroll down and click "Commit changes".
13. You should now see all six files listed on the repo page. If any is
    missing, repeat step 10 for that file.


PART 4 — TURN ON HOSTING
------------------------
14. Click "Settings" (top bar of the repo, on the right, with a gear icon).
15. In the LEFT sidebar, scroll down and click "Pages".
16. Under "Build and deployment" > "Source", leave it as
    "Deploy from a branch".
17. Under "Branch", the dropdown probably says "None". Change it to "main".
    Leave the folder dropdown as "/ (root)".
18. Click "Save".
19. Wait about one to two minutes, then reload the Settings > Pages page.
    A green box appears with your address:

        https://YOURNAME.github.io/surgimaster/

    (with your username in place of YOURNAME)

    If it still says "Your site is being built", wait another minute and
    reload. First deploy is the slow one.


PART 5 — INSTALL IT ON THE PHONE
--------------------------------
20. Open that address in SAFARI on the iPhone or iPad.
    It MUST be Safari. Chrome on iOS cannot add apps to the home screen.
21. Check the app loads and you can see the schedule.
22. Tap the SHARE button — the square with an arrow pointing up, in the
    bottom bar (or top right on iPad).
23. Scroll down the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen".
24. The name will already say SurgiMaster. Tap "Add" (top right).
25. Close Safari. Open SurgiMaster from your home screen.

It should now open full screen with no address bar. Put it in flight mode and
open it again to confirm it works offline — it will.


PART 6 — THE CALENDAR
---------------------
Do this once, BEFORE importing anything:
    Calendar app > Calendars > Add Calendar > name it "SurgiMaster".

Everything then lands in that one calendar, so you can hide the entire
schedule with one tap, or delete the calendar to remove all 1,893 entries at
once. Import into your default calendar and you will be deleting events one
at a time.

Then either open the .ics file from the Files app, or — better — open the app,
go to Plan > "Add to your calendar", choose your options and tap "Open in
Calendar". Exporting from the app means the calendar always matches the
schedule on screen, including any pacing changes you have made since.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
"404 — There isn't a GitHub Pages site here"
    Either Pages is not switched on (redo Part 4), or the files went into a
    subfolder instead of the top level. Open the repo: you should see
    index.html listed directly, not inside another folder.

The address works but the page is blank
    The upload missed index.html. Repeat Part 3 for that file.

"Add to Home Screen" is not in the share sheet
    You are not in Safari. Copy the address and open it in Safari.

The icon is a grey page instead of the SurgiMaster icon
    icon.png did not upload. Add it, then remove the home-screen shortcut and
    add it again.

I changed something and the app still looks old
    Installed apps cache aggressively. Delete the home-screen icon, open the
    address in Safari again, then re-add it.


UPDATING LATER
--------------
If I send you a new version, open the repo, click the old file, click the
pencil or the "..." > "Upload files", replace it, and commit. The live site
updates within a minute. Your logged data is untouched by this — it lives on
the phone, not in the repo. Back it up anyway from Diary > Backup first.


A NOTE ON PRIVACY
-----------------
The repo is public, so the app code is visible to anyone with the link. The
code contains your curriculum structure (topic names, lecture counts, question
counts) and nothing else. No misses, no scores, no calibration data, no name.
All of that is stored only in your phone browser and is never sent anywhere —
the app makes no network requests of any kind.
